# Idea District — screen flow (Abdul's spec) and menu bar

> Source: pasted by Pravat in chat on 2026-10-22. Kept verbatim apart from markdown formatting.

## 01. Loading page — `01_Loading`
Very minimal. Logo: **ID**, then **Idea District**. Small line: *Understanding before recommendation.* Maybe a subtle animated network of dots.
After 1–2 seconds → next screen: "Let's discover your ID."

## 02. Intro / entry — `02_Intro`
Headline: **You are more than your profile.**
Subheading: Your ID isn't something you fill out. It's something we discover through your journey.
[ Begin Discovery ] — small line: Takes about 5 minutes.
Purpose: set the expectation that this isn't another registration form.

## 03. Basic identity — `03_Identity`
Don't ask 20 things. Just establish who we're talking to.
Fields: Name · What are you currently working on? · Organisation / Company · Optional: LinkedIn / website
[ Continue ]

## 04. Current context — `04_Context` (VERY important)
Headline: **Where are you right now?** Ask 3–4 questions.
- Q1 What are you trying to make happen right now? — Build something · Find customers · Raise capital · Find the right people · Change direction · Learn something · Find an opportunity · Other
- Q2 What's currently getting in the way? — Capital · People · Knowledge · Customers · Technology · Time · Direction · Access · Nothing specific
- Q3 What would be most useful to you right now? — A person · An idea · A capability · An introduction · Knowledge · An opportunity
[ Continue ]

## 05. Discovery intro — `05_Discovery`
Now we transition from form into intelligence.
Headline: **Now let's go a little deeper.** Subtext: There are things about you that a profile can't tell us.
We'll give you a few situations. Your choices help us understand how you think, what you value and what you're trying to achieve.
[ Start Discovery ]

## 06. Simulation / situational questions — `06_Simulation`
The heart of the prototype. Do NOT make this look like a personality test. Don't say "Are you a risk taker?" — give situations.
Example: *You have ₹50 lakh to put into your business. Your product is working, but growth has stalled. What do you do first?*
A: Hire a stronger team. B: Spend on customer acquisition. C: Change the product. D: Find a strategic partner. E: Keep the money and investigate why growth stalled.
After selection: [ Continue ]. The next situation can change based on the previous answer. That's important.

## 07. Adaptive discovery — `07_Simulation_Next`
Should feel different from a questionnaire. Example: *You chose to investigate before spending. You discover that customers love the product, but your sales process is weak. What would you do next?* Options change.
Demonstrates **Choice → Signal → Next Situation** instead of Question 1 → Question 2 → Question 3.

## 08. Final discovery — `08_DiscoveryComplete`
After maybe 5–7 situations. Headline: **We've learned enough to start.** Then: Your ID is taking shape. Animation: Signals → ID. [ See My ID ]

## 09. ID generation — `09_ID_Building`
Visually impressive transition. Show: Understanding ↓ Capabilities ↓ Motivations ↓ Behaviour ↓ Current State ↓ ID.
Could animate the user's information forming a network.
Text sequence: Building your ID… → Understanding your current state… → Looking for potential intersections…
Should feel like an intelligence system rather than a form.

## 10. My ID — `10_My_ID`
First major output. Not a boring profile card — an ID map. Example:

- **ABDUL**
- Current State: Building Idea District
- Direction: Intelligence • Entrepreneurship • Systems
- Capabilities: Strategy · Brand · Systems Thinking · Product Thinking
- Current Needs: Technical development · Investor ecosystem · Product validation
- Interests: Energy · Mobility · Deep Tech · Infrastructure
- Signals: Strategic · High curiosity · Cross-domain thinker · Pattern-oriented
- Confidence: **Developing** — because the system doesn't know everything yet. The ID should feel alive and incomplete.

## Menu bar (four tabs, as discussed)
1. **Profile** — to edit personal info
2. **Messaging**
3. **Recommendations**
4. **Whiteboard** — to post real-world problems

"We will track each of them and every interaction will evolve their personal ID."
